# M024 (L7): "Mouawad Blue" (Tereschenko), 42.92 ct fancy blue pear diamond: $4,600,000 (Christie's Geneva, November 1984)

## Caption

ماسة زرقاء بـ4.6 مليون دولار لصائغ لبناني 🔨

## Sources (re-opened 15 Nov 2026)

- Mouawad, Diamond Gallery "Mouawad Blue" (official): originally the Tereschenko Diamond; second-largest blue diamond in the world after the Hope; 42.92 ct, pear, Fancy Blue; set by Cartier in a necklace around 1913; location a mystery for decades until it reappeared at a Christie's auction in Geneva in 1984; GIA-certified; "purchased by Robert Mouawad for $4.6 million, which at the time was the highest price ever paid for a diamond at auction". https://www.mouawad.com/hk-en/diamond-gallery-mouawad-blue.html
- Gem-A (Jack Ogden FGA), 2 Feb 2017: 42.92 ct; "sold at Christie's in Geneva in November 1984 for a then-record price of $4.6 million to Robert Mouawad and is now called the Mouawad Blue"; first known appearance 1913, left with Cartier in Paris by Mikhail Tereschenko. https://gem-a.com/gem-hub/mouawad-tereschenko-diamond-second-largest-blue-diamond/

Exact day of the sale not found; card and caption say "نوفمبر 1984". "More than a hundred years ago" = set by Cartier c. 1913 (Mouawad) / instructed to mount 1915 (Gem-A).
