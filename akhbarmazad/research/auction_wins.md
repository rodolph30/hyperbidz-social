## How to read this file

- **Confidence.** *Verified* means two or more reputable outlets, or one official source (organiser, government news agency such as WAM, SPA or QNA, or an auction-house release). *Partly verified* means only one reputable outlet, or a reputable outlet relaying a local paper. Every source listed was opened during this research: the page text was retrieved and read.
- **Buyer names.** A buyer is named only when a reputable outlet or the organiser named that buyer. Minors who appear in the coverage are never named.
- **USD conversion.** Pegged rates: AED 3.6725, SAR 3.75, QAR 3.64, BHD 0.376 and OMR 0.3845 per USD. The Kuwaiti dinar floats against a basket, so it is approximated at 1 KWD ≈ 3.26 USD, alongside the figure the source gave at the time. Guineas use the conversion published by the source. Old sales are converted at today's pegged rate.
- **Price basis.** Prices are hammer or "sold" prices as reported. Auction-house results (Sotheby's, RM Sotheby's) include buyer's premium. Dubai RTA plate prices exclude 5% VAT. Saudi plate prices exclude 15% VAT unless stated.
- **Photos.** We do not reuse photos without permission. Each entry notes who appears to own the official images.

### 1. Dubai plate "P 7": AED 55 million
- **EN headline:** Two characters, AED 55 million: Dubai's "P 7" becomes the world's most expensive number plate
- **AR headline:** «P 7» تُباع بـ55 مليون درهم وتصبح أغلى لوحة سيارة في العالم

- **Price:** AED 55,000,000 (≈ US$14.98M at 3.6725).
