## How to read this file

- **Confidence.** *Verified* means an official source (the auction house's own lot page, results PDF or press release; the operator's own press release; the committee statement as carried by NNA) or two reputable outlets. *Partly verified* means one reputable outlet only. Every source listed was opened during this research and its text was read.
- **Buyer names.** A buyer is named only when a reputable outlet or the organiser named them. Everyone else is "undisclosed" (مزايد لم يُكشف اسمه).
- **Prices.** Auction-house prices include buyer's premium (Christie's, Sotheby's and Bonhams publish them that way). Lebanese plate prices are the committee's announced "sum collected" per plate.
- **USD conversions.**
  - GBP lots: the USD figure is the one the cited outlet published (The National, Artnet, Gulf Today, AD Middle East, HENI News or Artprice). Where none was published, the entry says so and gives our own approximate rate.
  - Lebanese pounds (2022): the parallel-market rate published by the961 on the day of the session (7 July 2022: LBP 29,200–29,250 per $; 30 June 2022: LBP 28,150–28,200 per $). The official peg was still LBP 1,507.5 at the time; the entry gives that figure too, for context only. Use the market-rate figure on the card.
  - CHF (2000): our approximation of about CHF 1.75 per $ (no source published a USD figure).
- **Photos.** Auction-house lot images are © Christie's Images Ltd / Sotheby's. Ask the house before use, or credit "الصورة: كريستيز" / "الصورة: سوذبيز" if the owner approves. No Getty, Reuters, AFP or AP images were found among the photos listed. Each entry says what the page's `og:image` actually shows. The tags were read in the Higgsfield sandbox, and the key images were viewed.
- **Rules check.** No item below involves politics, religion, war or a private person's misfortune *if the caption sticks to the facts given*. Where an artwork's title or catalogue text touches a sensitive theme, the entry says what to leave out.

### 1. "Mouawad Splendor": $12.76 million
- **EN headline:** A Lebanese jeweller paid a world-record $12.76m for one diamond "the size of a quail's egg"
- **AR headline:** صائغ لبناني يشتري ماسة واحدة بـ12.76 مليون دولار... رقم قياسي عالمي
- **Item:** The 101.84-carat, D-colour, internally flawless, 11-sided pear-shaped diamond, later named the "Mouawad Splendor".
- **Winner:** **Robert Mouawad**, Lebanese jeweller (Mouawad, founded in Beirut in 1890–91). His son represented him in the room; the son is not named in the coverage.
- **Price:** $12,760,000 (the sale was reported in USD). A 1995 UPI report gives the figure as "$12.6 million". Use $12.76M, the figure Mouawad and the 1990 report give.
- **Where/when:** Sotheby's, Geneva. Wednesday 14 November 1990.
- **Wow detail:** The 1990 report called it "the largest, purest diamond ever auctioned" and a world record. Mouawad says it won the company's first Guinness World Record, as the most expensive single pear-shaped diamond.
- **Sources:** [Orlando Sentinel, 15 Nov 1990 (wire report)](https://www.orlandosentinel.com/1990/11/15/diamond-brings-13-million-its-as-big-as-quails-egg/); [Mouawad, World Records (official)](https://www.mouawad.com/int-en/world-records-mouawad-splendor.html); also [CNBC, 2013](https://www.cnbc.com/2013/07/24/The-worlds-richest-diamond-owners.html) and [UPI, 1995](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1995/05/17/Saudi-sheik-buys-worlds-most-expensive-jewel/4002800683200).
- **Confidence:** Verified
- **Photo:** Mouawad's own World Records page shows the stone (image owned by Mouawad; our fetch could not read its meta tags, so ask Mouawad for a press image). CNBC used a photo credited "www.mouawad.com". The Orlando Sentinel page has no photo (its og:image is a site fallback logo).
- **Re-checked 26 Oct 2026 (M004):** Mouawad's page (fetched in the Higgsfield sandbox with a cookie jar; it redirect-loops without one) shows the Guinness certificate: "sold for £8,100,000 ($12,760,000) to Robert Mouawad, at Sotheby's, Geneva, Switzerland, on 14 Nov 1990". Usable photos: `World%20Records/mouawad-splendor-banner-video-poster.jpg` (2560x1180, stone on black; used on M004) and `World%20Records/NLGy6jjI.jpeg` (935x924, stone on white).
