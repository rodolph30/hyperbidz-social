# Lesson 10 · The first number wins · style v3 · quick · ~31 s · am_michael 1.08 · music: lofi, seed 10 · who: Sami (seller)

Arc: problem → reframe → fix → takeaway (owner, 23 Oct 2026).

- That lowball offer on your car? It just set your price.
- In three experiments, whoever named the first number got the better deal.
- The first offer was a strong predictor of the final price.
- Even real-estate agents were pulled by the list price, and denied it.
- A lowball isn't rude. It's an anchor.
- In an auction, the buyers name the numbers, and each one is higher.
- In a chat, the first number pulls the price down. In an auction, every number pushes it up.
- Follow for more interesting auction stories.
