# Lesson 10 v2 captions

## Option 1
You list your car for $9,000. The first message says $4,500. ⚓

You answer $8,000. They say $6,000. You settle at $7,000.
That lowball wasn't rude. It was an anchor: the first number pulls the whole deal toward it.
In negotiation experiments, whoever named the first number got the better deal.
In an auction, the buyers name the numbers, and every bid must be higher.

Source: Galinsky & Mussweiler, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology (2001). Prices in the video are illustrative.
