## Option 1

That lowball offer on your car? It just set your price. ⚓

In three experiments, whoever named the first number got the better deal.
The first offer was a strong predictor of the final price.
Even real-estate agents were pulled by a list price, and denied it.
A lowball isn't rude. It's an anchor.
In an auction, the buyers name the numbers, and each one is higher than the last.

Source: Galinsky & Mussweiler, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology (2001); Northcraft & Neale (1987). Prices in the video are illustrative.
