# Lesson 05 quiz (optional story Quiz sticker)

**In a Dutch auction, who wins?**
- A. The highest bidder at the end
- B. The first buyer to accept the falling price ✅
- C. A random bidder
- D. The seller chooses

Extra: In Lebanon, if nobody bids at a court sale, the price is cut and the sale is tried again 15 to 30 days later, until a buyer comes forward (Code of Civil Procedure, Art. 931).
