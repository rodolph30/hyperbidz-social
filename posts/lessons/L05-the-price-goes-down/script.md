# Lesson 05 · The auction where the price goes down · style v3 · quick · 35.5 s · am_michael 1.08 · music: lofi, seed 5

- This auction's price goes down, not up.
- And Lebanon has a slow-motion version.
- In Holland, flowers sell on a clock that counts the price down.
- The first to press wins. Too soon, you overpay. Too late, they're gone.
- Last year, almost 5 billion flowers and plants sold this way.
- Flowers can't wait, and a falling price sells fast.
- In Lebanon, if no one bids at a court sale, the price is cut,
- and the sale comes back 15 to 30 days later, until someone bids.
- Either way, the price comes down to meet a buyer.
- Follow for more interesting auction stories.
