# Lesson 05 · The auction where the price goes down

## Option 1
This auction's price goes DOWN, not up. ⏬

In Holland, flowers are sold on a clock: the price starts high and falls, and the first buyer to press wins.
Press too soon and you overpay. Too late and someone else gets the flowers.
In 2025, almost 5 billion flowers and plants were traded on the clock. It's known worldwide as a Dutch auction.
Lebanon has a slow-motion version: if no one bids at a court sale, the price is cut and the sale comes back 15 to 30 days later, again and again, until someone buys.

Sources: Royal FloraHolland; Lebanese Code of Civil Procedure, Art. 931.
Would you press early, or wait for a lower price? 👇

## Option 2
The first to press wins. But press too soon and you overpay. 🌷

That's the Dutch flower auction: a clock counts the price down until a buyer stops it. Almost 5 billion flowers and plants went through it in 2025.
In Lebanon, a court sale with no bidders works the same way, just slower: the price is cut and the sale is tried again 15 to 30 days later.

Sources: Royal FloraHolland; Lebanese Code of Civil Procedure, Art. 931.
