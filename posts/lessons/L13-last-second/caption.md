# Lesson 13 captions

## Option 1
Three days in the lead. Lost in the last second. ⏱️

Nour led an online auction for three days. One second before the end, someone outbid her. No time to answer.
Economists studied 240 online auctions with a fixed end time: 89 got bids in the last minute, 29 in the last ten seconds.
Then they looked at auctions where a late bid adds time. Bids in the last minute: just one.
The last-second win wasn't skill. It was the rule.
A fair finish gives every bid an answer.

Source: Roth & Ockenfels, "Last-Minute Bidding and the Rules for Ending Second-Price Auctions", American Economic Review (2002). The item, bids and names in the video are illustrative.
Have you ever lost something in the last second? 👇

## Option 2
A fair finish gives every bid an answer. ⏱️

In 240 online auctions with a fixed end, 29 got bids in the last ten seconds. Where a late bid added time, just one bid came in the last minute.
When time is added after a late bid, everyone gets a chance to bid back.

Source: Roth & Ockenfels, American Economic Review (2002).
Send this to someone who lost an auction in the last second.
