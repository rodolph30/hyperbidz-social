# Lesson 13 quiz (optional story sticker)

**Question:** What stops a last-second bid from winning unanswered?
- Bidding early
- A higher starting price
- **A rule that adds time after a late bid** ✅
- Bidding from a computer

Extra: In 240 fixed-end online auctions, 89 got bids in the last minute and 29 in the last ten seconds; where the clock ran until ten minutes passed without a bid, only one bid came in the last minute (Roth & Ockenfels, American Economic Review, 2002).
