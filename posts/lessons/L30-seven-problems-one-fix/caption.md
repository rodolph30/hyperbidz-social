# Caption options (lesson 30)

## Option 1
7 buying and selling problems. 1 fix. How many have you lived?

A lowball first offer → let buyers compete.
"Is it still available?" → a countdown everyone sees.
No-shows → a small deposit.
Paying a stranger first → a track record you can check.
A hidden price → every bid in the open.

This isn't theory: 1,882 homes went to auction in one October week in Australia's capital cities, Japan puts about 8 million used cars up for auction a year, and Tripoli has sold its fish by auction since 1958.

So why do we still sell everything else by chat?

## Option 2
Lowballs. "Is it still available?" No-shows. "DM for price." Sound familiar?

Every one of these has the same fix: an auction. Buyers compete, a countdown makes people decide, a deposit keeps out time-wasters, a track record replaces blind trust, and every bid is in the open.

Australia sells homes this way, Japan sells used cars this way, and Tripoli has sold fish this way since 1958.
