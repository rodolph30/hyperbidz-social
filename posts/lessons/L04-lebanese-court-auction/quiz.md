# Lesson 04 quiz (optional story sticker)

Q: In a Lebanese court auction, the opening price is…
- 100% of the valuation
- 60% of the valuation ✅
- Whatever the judge likes
- The first bid

Extra: the law (CCP Art. 965) sets it at six tenths of the estimated value, and bidders must deposit that amount before bidding (Art. 973).
