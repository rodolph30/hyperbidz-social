# Lesson 20 captions

## Option 1
Haggling has a hidden price. And you've probably paid it. 🧾

Researchers ran five studies on how people negotiate.
95% of people chose not to negotiate, up to half the time.
On average, haggling only felt worth it to save 21–36% of the price.
So businesses can raise prices 5–11%, and more than half of people pay it.
Haggling has a price, and both sides pay it. In an auction, everyone bids in the open, by the same rules.

Source: Hunsaker, Zhang & Lee, Negotiation and Conflict Management Research (2025), via Indiana University. Names and prices in the video are illustrative.
