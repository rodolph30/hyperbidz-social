# Caption, option 1

In Tripoli, fish has been sold by auction since 1958. 🐟

Under stone arches about 400 years old.
And it's not the only one: there are fish auctions at the ports of Dora, Ouzai, Saida, Tripoli and Sour.
Restaurants, fishmongers and families bid on the same catch.
Fresh fish can't wait, so the bids find the price on the spot, in front of everyone.

Source: FAO, Fishery and Aquaculture Country Profile, Lebanon. The hall drawing, crate, bids and people in the video are illustrative.
Lebanon already sells its fish by auction. Fast, fair, in the open. Have you ever been to a fish auction? 👇
