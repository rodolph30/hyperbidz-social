# Story quiz (lesson 23)

**Why is fresh fish sold by auction?**
- It's the law
- It must sell fast, and the bids find the price ✓
- Fishermen don't like haggling
- It's only for tourists

Extra: FAO lists fish auctions at the ports of Dora, Ouzai, Saida, Tripoli and Sour. Tripoli's Sidawi auction was founded in 1958 in a building about 400 years old with stone arches (FAO, Lebanon country profile).
