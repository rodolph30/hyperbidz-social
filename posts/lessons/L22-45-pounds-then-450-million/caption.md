# Caption options (lesson 22)

## Option 1
The same painting: £45. Then $450 million. 🖼️

In 1958 it was mistaken for a copy, and it sold at auction for £45.
It resurfaced in 2005, then went through six years of cleaning and research.
In 2017 it came back to auction in New York. Bidding opened at $75 million.
19 minutes and at least 45 bids later: $450,312,500.
Nobody set that price. The bidders found it.

Source: Christie's (press release, 15 Nov 2017). Price includes fees. Sami's listing and prices in the video are illustrative.
Your price isn't what you ask. It's what people compete for. Let them bid. Have you ever sold something too cheap? 👇
