# Lesson 03 · The busiest minute on Wall Street (the closing auction)

## Option 1
The biggest trade of the day on Wall Street is an auction. 🔔

All day, stocks trade one by one: a bid meets an ask, a trade happens.
But at exactly 4 p.m., the New York Stock Exchange gathers every buy and sell order and finds the one price that matches the most shares. Everyone trades at that price, in one go. It's called the closing auction.
On a normal day in 2024, US closing auctions matched about $50 billion, roughly 9% of all trading.
On March 20, 2026, one NYSE closing auction matched a record 3.57 billion shares.

Sources: BMLL "Into the Close" (2025); NYSE "Behind the Record Volumes" (2026).
Did you know the closing price was set by an auction? 👇

## Option 2
That closing price you see on the news? An auction set it. 📈

Every weekday at 4 p.m., the NYSE stops trading one by one and runs a single auction: all orders in, one price out.
About $50 billion a day goes through US closing auctions (2024). The record: 3.57 billion shares in one auction (March 20, 2026).
Auctions don't just sell paintings. They price the world's biggest companies.

Sources: BMLL (2025); NYSE (2026).
