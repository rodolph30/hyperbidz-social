# Lesson 03 · The busiest minute on Wall Street · style v3 · 56 s · narrator am_michael at 1.07 · music: ambient, seed 3

- The biggest trade of the day on Wall Street is an auction.
- And it happens at exactly 4 p.m.
- All day long, stocks trade one by one.
- Nour bids $101. Sami asks $102.
- When a bid meets an ask, a trade happens.
- But at 4 p.m., the New York Stock Exchange does something different.
- It gathers every buy order and every sell order,
- and finds the one price that matches the most shares.
- Everyone trades at that price. In one go.
- It's called the closing auction.
- On a normal day in 2024, US closing auctions matched about $50 billion.
- And on March 20, 2026, one of them matched 3.57 billion shares.
- So that closing price you see on the news?
- An auction set it.
- Auctions don't just sell paintings. They price the world's biggest companies.
- Follow to learn how auctions really work.
