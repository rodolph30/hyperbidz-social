# Lesson 09 captions

## Option 1

In Lebanon, you can win a court auction and still lose it. ⚖️

Lebanese law gives the winner of a court property auction three days to deposit the full price.
If the money isn't there, a new session is announced, naming the first buyer and the price.
At that session, anyone who brings the price + 10% (in full, or a bank guarantee) reopens the bidding.
If nobody does, the first buyer can still pay, with the cost of the delay. If not, it goes straight back to auction.
The rule keeps the sale moving: winning is step one, paying is what makes it yours.

Source: Lebanese Code of Civil Procedure, Art. 978 (legiliban.ul.edu.lb). Prices in the video are illustrative.

## Option 2

Win the auction. Miss the payment. Lose it to +10%. ⚖️

3 days to deposit the full price.
Then a new session: anyone can take over with 10% more, cash or bank guarantee in hand.
Nobody comes? The first buyer gets one last chance to pay.

Source: Lebanese Code of Civil Procedure, Art. 978.
