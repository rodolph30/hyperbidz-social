# Lesson 09 · The Lebanese 10% rule (زيادة العشر) · style v3 · standard · 53.0 s · am_michael 1.07 · music: pluck, seed 9

- In Lebanon, you can win a court auction and still lose it, to a stranger with 10% more.
- It all comes down to three days.
- Say Nour wins an apartment at a court auction for $100,000.
- The judge awards it, and a clock starts.
- She has three days to deposit the full price.
- If she doesn't, there's a new session, announced again with her name and the price.
- Now anyone can come and offer 10% more: $110,000.
- Only with the full amount in hand, or a bank guarantee.
- Then the bidding starts again from there.
- If nobody comes, Nour can still pay, plus the cost of the delay, in that session.
- If not, the apartment goes straight back to auction.
- That's the point: a winner who doesn't pay can't freeze the sale.
- Winning is step one. Paying is what makes it yours.
- Follow for more interesting auction stories.
