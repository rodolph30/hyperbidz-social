# Lesson 11 quiz (optional story sticker)

**Question:** Same seller, same postcards. How much more did buyers pay when he sold under his established name?
- 0%
- **8.1%** ✅
- 50%
- Double

Extra: A high-reputation dealer sold matched pairs of vintage-postcard lots under his regular identity and under new identities he also ran; buyers' willingness to pay differed by 8.1% of the selling price (Resnick et al., 2006).
