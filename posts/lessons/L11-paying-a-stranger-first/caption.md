# Lesson 11 captions

## Option 1
"Send the money first." Would you? 💸

Nour found a phone online. The seller had no reviews and no history.
Researchers tested how much a track record is worth: one dealer sold matching lots of postcards under his own established name and under brand-new names.
Same seller, same postcards. Buyers paid 8.1% more to the name with a track record.
An online auction can show every seller's past sales and reviews, for anyone to check.
Trust isn't a feeling. It's a track record you can check.

Source: Resnick, Zeckhauser, Swanson & Lockwood, Experimental Economics (2006). The phone, price and seller in the video are illustrative.
