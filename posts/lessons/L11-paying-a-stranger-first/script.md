# Lesson 11 · Paying a stranger first · style v3 · quick · ~36 s · am_michael 1.08 · music: keys, seed 11 · who: Nour (buyer)

Built like L10 v2 (the owner's "one story, one fact" note): Nour's phone purchase runs from the first frame to the last, and one sourced fact carries the reframe. The FTC and ISF items listed for this lesson (P7) were left out on purpose to keep it to one fact.

Arc: problem → reframe → fix → takeaway.

- Nour finds a phone online. The seller says: send the money first.
- She's never met him. No reviews. No history. Would you pay?
- Researchers tested it. One dealer sold matching postcards under his own name, and under new names.
- Same seller, same postcards. Buyers paid 8.1% more to the name with a track record.
- An online auction can show every seller's past sales and reviews, for anyone to check.
- Trust isn't a feeling. It's a track record you can check. Before you send the money first.
- Follow for more interesting auction stories.
