# Lesson 11 sources (re-checked 26 Oct 2026)

- Resnick, P., Zeckhauser, R., Swanson, J. & Lockwood, K. (2006). "The value of reputation on eBay: A controlled experiment." Experimental Economics 9(2). Abstract: "A high-reputation, established eBay dealer sold matched pairs of lots—batches of vintage postcards—under his regular identity and under new seller identities (also operated by him). As predicted, the established identity fared better. The difference in buyers' willingness-to-pay was 8.1% of the selling price."
