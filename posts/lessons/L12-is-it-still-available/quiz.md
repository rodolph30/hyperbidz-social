# Lesson 12 quiz (optional story sticker)

**Question:** In bargaining experiments with a deadline, when did 41% of the deals happen?
- In the first minute
- Halfway through
- **In the last 30 seconds** ✅
- After the deadline

Extra: Across four experiments, 41% of agreements came in the last 30 seconds, and 53% of those in the last 5 seconds (Roth, Murnighan & Schoumaker, American Economic Review, 1988).
