# Lesson 12 captions

## Option 1
"Is it still available?" …and then nothing. 📱

Sami's bike gets the same message every day. "I'll think about it." "Can you hold it till next week?" Nobody buys.
Researchers watched people bargain against a deadline in four experiments.
41% of the deals came in the last 30 seconds, and more than half of those in the last 5 seconds.
People don't decide when they're ready. They decide when time runs out.
An auction gives everyone the same countdown. At zero, it's decided.
No deadline, no decision.

Source: Roth, Murnighan & Schoumaker, "The Deadline Effect in Bargaining", American Economic Review (1988). The bike, price and messages in the video are illustrative.

## Option 2
No deadline, no decision. ⏱️

In four bargaining experiments, 41% of deals came in the last 30 seconds before the deadline, and more than half of those in the last 5.
A countdown everyone can see is what turns "maybe" into a decision.

Source: Roth, Murnighan & Schoumaker, American Economic Review (1988).
