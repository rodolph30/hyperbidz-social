## Option 1
This fish sold for $3.2 million. 🐟

Tokyo, 5 January 2026: the year's first auction at Toyosu market.
A 243 kg bluefin tuna from Oma.
The winning sushi chain expected to pay 300 to 400 million yen. The bids climbed to a record 510.3 million.
"The price shot up before you knew it," said the buyer. It was partly for good luck, and it was served at the usual menu price.
At an auction, the price isn't a label. It's how much someone wants it.

Sources: AP; Kyodo; Nippon TV (5 Jan 2026).
