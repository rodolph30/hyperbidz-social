# Story quiz (lesson 26)

**In the study of real online auctions, what did lower starting prices lead to?**
- Fewer bidders
- Lower final prices
- More bidders and higher final prices ✓
- No difference

Extra: Ku, Galinsky & Murnighan (2006) looked at eBay auctions for 179 Persian rugs and 87 Nikon cameras. Lower starts drew more bids and bidders and ended higher, except in listings with a misspelled name, where low starts ended low.
