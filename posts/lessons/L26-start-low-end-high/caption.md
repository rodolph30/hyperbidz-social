# Caption options (lesson 26)

## Option 1
Want a higher price at auction? Start lower. 📈

Sounds backwards, but researchers tested it on real online auctions: 179 Persian rugs and 87 cameras.
The lower the starting price, the more bidders joined, and the higher it ended.
Except when the listing's name was misspelled: few people found it, and a low start stayed low.
So start low, set a reserve as your floor, and make sure people can see it.
In a chat, start high. In an auction, start low and let them bid.

Source: Ku, Galinsky & Murnighan, "Starting low but ending high", Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 2006. Sami's rug and the bids in the video are illustrative.
Would you dare start your auction at $1? 👇

## Option 2
A low starting price can mean a higher final price. 🤯

In real online auctions of rugs and cameras, lower starts brought more bidders and higher prices (Ku, Galinsky & Murnighan, 2006). It only works when people can find it.

Source: Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 2006.
