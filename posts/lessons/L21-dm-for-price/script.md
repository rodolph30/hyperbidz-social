# Lesson 21 · "DM for price" (quick, ~35 s, music keys seed 21, who: Nour)

Problem → reframe → fix → takeaway. The listing, the $700,000 / $210,000 / $185,000 prices, the chat and the bids are illustrative and labelled on screen.

| Scene | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Hide the price, and in one place, the auction can't go ahead. The reason is the best part. | A home listing, "Reserve price: DM" in a red selection box, NO AUCTION stamp, a "Why?" pill |
| Problem | Nour asks the price. The reply: DM. Then her friend gets a different number. | Dark chat "Apartment for sale": "How much?" / "DM" / "$210,000", her friend's card "was told $185,000", pill "Different number" |
| Reframe | In Victoria, Australia, a home's minimum price must be public 7 days before its auction. Or no auction. | Stat card "Reserve price public: 7 days, before a home auction", chip "Victoria, Australia · since Oct 2026", OR NO AUCTION stamp |
| Payoff (the reason) | Why? So buyers stop finding out the house was never in their price range. | Dark quote card with the government's words from Hansard ("…was never in their price range."), Nour reacts |
| Fix | An open auction fixes that. Every bid is public. Same number, for everyone. | "Open auction" window with four bid rows, chip "Every bid public", pill "Same number, for everyone" |
| Takeaway + loop | When the price is public, nobody gets a special price. | Back to the opening listing: "DM" turns into "$700,000" (green box), pill "No special prices" |
| End card | Follow for more interesting auction stories. | Paddles, Sami and Nour, "+ Follow" clicked |
