# Lesson 21 captions

## Option 1
Hide the price, and in one place, the auction can't go ahead. 🏠

"DM for price." Then a friend gets a different number.
In Victoria, Australia, since October 2026, a home's reserve price must be published at least 7 days before the auction, as one dollar amount. If it isn't, the auction can't go ahead.
After the sale, the sold price must be published too, and stay public for 18 months.
The government's reason: to end the frustration of buyers who learn "the house was never in their price range."
In an open auction, every bid is public: the same number, for everyone.

Source: Consumer Affairs Victoria; Parliament of Victoria (Hansard, 14 Aug 2026). Names and prices in the video are illustrative.

## Option 2
"DM for price"? In one place, that's now a reason to stop the auction. 📩

In Victoria, Australia, a home's minimum price must be public 7 days before the auction, or the auction can't go ahead. When the price is public, nobody gets a special price.

Source: Consumer Affairs Victoria (2026).
