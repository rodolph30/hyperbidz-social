# Lesson 21 sources (re-checked 5 Nov 2026)

- Consumer Affairs Victoria, "New changes to property sales and underquoting laws":
  - Agents "must publish the seller's reserve price at least 7 days before an auction or fixed-date sale", "in a single dollar amount, without words or symbols such as 'from', 'over', '+' or 'starting at'."
  - "If the reserve price hasn't been published 7 days before the auction or fixed-date sale, it cannot go ahead."
  - In effect from 1 October 2026, for auctions and fixed-date sales on and from 16 October 2026.
  - Sale price: the Property Price Statement must be updated with the sale price 7 days after the unconditional sale and stay public for at least 18 months (caption only).
  - https://www.consumer.vic.gov.au/licensing-and-registration/estate-agents/new-changes-to-property-sales-and-underquoting-laws
- Parliament of Victoria, Hansard, 14 Aug 2026, Consumer Legislation Amendment Bill 2026 (Ingrid Stitt, speaking for the government):
  - "The purpose of these reforms is to end the frustration that prospective buyers feel when they go to an auction and learn that the house was never in their price range."
  - https://www.parliament.vic.gov.au/parliamentary-activity/hansard/hansard-details/HANSARD-974425065-36367
