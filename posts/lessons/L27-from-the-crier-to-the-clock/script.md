# Lesson 27 · From the crier to the clock (quick, ~37 s, music: marimba seed 27, who: perspective, Lebanon)

Perspective lesson: what makes an auction fair is that it's public.

1. A **crier** still has a job in Lebanese law.
2. And his job is the secret of every fair **auction.**
3. To auction seized goods, a court officer may call in **the municipality's crier,** or another he picks.
4. For a property, the notice goes in **two newspapers,** the Official Gazette, and on the **municipality's door.**
5. At least **ten days** before.
6. Why all that noise? A price is only fair when **everyone** can bid.
7. Online, the crier becomes a **countdown** everyone can watch, from anywhere.
8. An auction is only as fair as it is **public.**
9. Follow for more **interesting auction stories.**
