# Story quiz (lesson 27)

**What was the crier's job at an auction?**
- To set the price
- To make the sale public, so everyone can bid ✓
- To guard the goods
- To collect the deposit

Extra: Lebanon's Code of Civil Procedure still lets a court officer call in "the municipality's crier" to auction seized goods (Art. 930), and a property auction must be announced in two daily newspapers, the Official Gazette and on the municipality's door, at least ten days before (Arts. 970–971).
