# Sources (lesson 27, re-checked 11 Nov 2026)

- Lebanese Code of Civil Procedure, Art. 930 (sale of seized movables): "يباشر مأمور التنفيذ اجراءات المزايدة في الموعد والمكان المعينين ويحيل المال على الزائد الاخير الذي يدفع الثمن الاكبر. ويمكن الاستعانة بدلال البلدية او بدلال آخر يختاره مأمور التنفيذ…" (the execution officer runs the bidding, awards to the last, highest bidder, and may call on the municipality's crier or another crier he picks). http://legiliban.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=260002&lawId=244565
- Lebanese Code of Civil Procedure, Arts. 970–971 (property auction): the notice is published in two daily newspapers chosen by the head of the Execution Department and in the Official Gazette, and posted on the department's board, the debtor's home, the property's door, "باب دار البلدية" (the municipality's door, or the mukhtar's house), and the town square, "قبل موعد جلسة المزايدة بعشرة ايام على الاقل" (at least ten days before the session). http://legiliban.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=260008&lawId=244565
