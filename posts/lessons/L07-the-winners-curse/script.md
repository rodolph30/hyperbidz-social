# Lesson 07 · The winner's curse · style v3 · quick · 37.9 s · am_michael 1.08 · music: marimba, seed 7

- This jar of coins was worth $8.
- The winners paid, on average, $10.01.
- Business students in Boston bid on jars like this, each writing a secret bid.
- Their average guess was low: just $5.13.
- But the winner is usually the one who guessed highest of all.
- So the winner lost $2.01, on average.
- In 1971, oil engineers gave it a name: the winner's curse.
- Beat fifty other bidders? How should you feel? Their answer:
- "Ill."
- The fix: decide your maximum before the bidding starts.
- Follow for more interesting auction stories.
