- Bulow, J. & Klemperer, P. (1996). "Auctions versus Negotiations." American Economic Review 86(1), 180–194. Abstract: "We show that under standard assumptions the public auction is always preferable, even if it forfeits all the seller's negotiating power, including the ability to withdraw the object from sale, provided that it attracts at least one extra bidder." … "They suggest that the value of negotiating skill is small relative to the value of additional competition."
