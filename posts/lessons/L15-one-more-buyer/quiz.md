# Lesson 15 quiz (optional story sticker)

**Question:** According to economists Bulow and Klemperer, what's worth more to a seller than negotiating skill?
- A higher asking price
- **One extra bidder** ✅
- A longer chat
- A lower first offer

Extra: Under standard assumptions, a simple public auction beats the best possible negotiation, provided it attracts at least one extra bidder (Bulow & Klemperer, American Economic Review, 1996).
