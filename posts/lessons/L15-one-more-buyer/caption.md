## Option 1
Better at haggling, and still getting less? 🤝

Sami sells his camera one buyer at a time. $500. $460. $420. Every chat starts from zero.
In 1996, two economists compared the best possible negotiation with a simple auction.
Their result: the auction wins, as long as it brings in just one extra buyer.
In their words, "the value of negotiating skill is small relative to the value of additional competition."
One more buyer beats the best negotiator. Let them bid.

Source: Bulow & Klemperer, "Auctions versus Negotiations", American Economic Review (1996). The camera, prices and chats in the video are illustrative.
