# Caption (lesson 29)

## Option 1

The best sellers never pick the price. The buyers do. 📈

1. Start low. On real online auctions for 179 rugs and 87 cameras, lower starts drew more bidders and ended higher.
2. Set a reserve: your floor. If the bids don't reach it, it doesn't sell.
3. Get one more buyer in. Economists showed one extra bidder beats the best negotiation.
4. Give it a deadline. In bargaining experiments, 41% of deals came in the last 30 seconds.
5. Let buyers from anywhere in. At Christie's, 81% of bids in 2024 were placed online.

Source: Ku, Galinsky & Murnighan, J. Pers. Soc. Psychol. (2006); Consumer Affairs Victoria; Bulow & Klemperer, American Economic Review (1996); Roth, Murnighan & Schoumaker, American Economic Review (1988); Christie's (Dec 2024). The camera, prices and timer in the video are illustrative.
Don't guess the price. Let them bid. Which rule surprised you? 👇
