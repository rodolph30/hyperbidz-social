Sami's five rules, one per beat, each carried by one sourced fact.

1. The best sellers never pick the price. **The buyers do.**
2. Sami has five rules. Rule one sounds **backwards** at first.
3. One: start **low.**
4. In real online auctions for rugs and cameras, lower starts drew more bidders, and **ended higher.**
5. Two: set a **reserve.** It's your floor. If the bids don't reach it, **it doesn't sell.**
6. Three: get **one more buyer** in. Economists proved one extra bidder beats the **best negotiation.**
7. Four: give it a **deadline.** In bargaining experiments, **41%** of deals came in the **last thirty seconds.**
8. Five: let buyers from **anywhere** in. At Christie's, **81%** of bids in 2024 were **placed online.**
9. Don't guess the price. Set the rules, and **let them bid.**
10. Follow for more **interesting auction stories.**
