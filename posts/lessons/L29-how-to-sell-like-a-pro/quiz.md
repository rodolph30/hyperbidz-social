# Story quiz (lesson 29)

**Why start an auction low?**
- To sell faster at any price
- It brings more bidders ✓
- Buyers trust low prices
- To skip the reserve

Extra: on real online auctions for 179 Persian rugs and 87 cameras, lower starting prices drew more bids and more bidders, and ended higher (Ku, Galinsky & Murnighan 2006). It only works when people can find the listing, and a reserve keeps the floor.
