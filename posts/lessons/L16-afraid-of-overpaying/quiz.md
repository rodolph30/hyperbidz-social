# Lesson 16 quiz (optional story sticker)

**Question:** At an open auction, the winner usually pays about…
- Her own maximum
- **One step above the runner-up's limit** ✅
- The starting price
- Double the starting price

Extra: In an open ascending auction, each bidder stops at their limit, so the highest-value bidder wins and pays about the second-highest bidder's value plus one bid increment (Vickrey 1961, as described by Lucking-Reiley, Journal of Economic Perspectives, 2000).
