# Lesson 17 captions

## Option 1
"I'm on my way." …and nobody came. 🕐

One booking platform checked its own numbers (Dec 2023 – Mar 2024):
With a hold on the card, 3% of bookings didn't show.
With a deposit, 1.7%. Fully prepaid, 0.9%.
The more people put down, the more they show up.
Lebanese court auctions use the same idea: to bid, you first deposit an amount equal to the full opening price.

Source: Tock (booking data, Dec 2023 – Mar 2024); Lebanese Code of Civil Procedure, Art. 973. The chat, item and bids in the video are illustrative.
Serious buyers don't mind a deposit. Time-wasters do. Has a buyer ever left you waiting? 👇
