# Lesson 17 quiz (optional story sticker)

**Question:** In one booking platform's data, which bookings had the fewest no-shows?
- A hold on the card
- A deposit
- **Fully prepaid** ✅
- No payment at all

Extra: From December 2023 through March 2024, Tock reported average no-show rates of 3% with credit card holds, 1.7% with deposits and 0.9% with full prepayment. In Lebanon, anyone who wants to bid at a court auction must first deposit an amount equal to the opening price (CCP Art. 973).
