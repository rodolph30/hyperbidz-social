# Lesson 14 captions

## Option 1
Last week, 1,882 homes went to auction in Australia's big cities. And that was a quiet week. 🏠

Here, "auction" sounds like trouble: a court sale, a seized apartment.
In Australia, it's a normal way to sell a home. In Sydney and Melbourne, most auctions are on a Saturday, some right in front of the house.
Buyers register, bids are called out loud, everyone hears every number.
If the bidding passes the seller's reserve, the highest bid wins. Not every home sells: last week, just under half did.

Source: Cotality (week ending 25 Oct 2026, preliminary); SQM Research (scheduled auctions, week ending 1 Nov 2026); NSW Government; Consumer Affairs Victoria. The bids and the house in the video are illustrative.
