# Lesson 14 · Australia sells its homes on Saturdays · style v3 · standard · ~43 s · am_michael 1.03 · music: pluck, seed 14 · who: perspective (Sami and Nour carry the "here" reaction)

A perspective lesson: it changes what the word "auction" means, from "trouble" to "a normal way to sell a home".

Arc: hook (a surprising number) → twist ("a quiet week") → the perspective (here vs there) → how it works (Saturday, in front of the house, open bids, a reserve) → honesty (not every home sells) → takeaway.

- Last week, 1,882 homes went to auction in Australia's big cities.
- And that was a quiet week. A year ago: 3,236.
- Here, the word "auction" sounds like trouble.
- A court sale. A seized apartment.
- There, it's a normal way to sell a home.
- And most of them happen on one day: Saturday.
- Some, right in front of the house.
- Buyers register. Bids are called out loud. Everyone hears every number.
- If the bidding passes the seller's reserve, the highest bid wins.
- Not every home is sold. Last week, just under half sold.
- Here, auction sounds like trouble. There, it sounds like Saturday.
- Follow for more interesting auction stories.
