# Lesson 24 · Your best buyer doesn't live on your street (quick, ~34 s, music: pluck seed 24, who: Sami)

Problem → reframe → fix → takeaway.

1. In 2024, **81%** of bids at Christie's were placed **online.**
2. Not in the room. And that's the answer to Sami's **problem.**
3. Sami posts his guitar. Only people **nearby** see it.
4. So his price is whatever his **street** will pay.
5. But Lebanon's economy ministry estimates **12 million** people of Lebanese origin live around the **world.**
6. Live online bidding lets buyers from **anywhere** compete for the same thing.
7. Your best buyer might not live on your street. They might be in **Sydney.**
8. Follow for more **interesting auction stories.**

Checklist: hook = a surprising number on frame 1 (81% card); open loop "the answer to Sami's problem"; problem lived (a post only nearby people see); one sourced reframe (81% online bids, Christie's) + the ministry's 12 million estimate, attributed; fix from the map (buyers from anywhere, live); takeaway flips perspective; loop: ends on the 81% card from frame 1.
