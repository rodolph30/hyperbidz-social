# Caption (lesson 28)

## Option 1

The smartest bidders decide everything before the first bid. 🧠

1. Check the seller's track record. In an eBay field experiment, the same dealer got 8.1% more under his established name.
2. Set your maximum before it starts. In open bidding you usually pay about one step above the runner-up, not your limit.
3. Know the reserve: the seller's floor. Below it, the item doesn't sell.
4. Don't panic at the end. When a late bid adds time, everyone gets to answer.
5. If you don't win, the deposit comes back. Lebanese court auctions do it: bidders who aren't the last bidder can withdraw it once the session ends.

Source: Resnick et al., Experimental Economics (2006); Lucking-Reiley, Journal of Economic Perspectives (2000); Consumer Affairs Victoria; Roth & Ockenfels, American Economic Review (2002); Lebanese Code of Civil Procedure, Arts. 973 and 977.
