# Sources (lesson 28, re-checked 12 Nov 2026)

- Rule 1. Resnick, Zeckhauser, Swanson & Lockwood, "The value of reputation on eBay: a controlled experiment", Experimental Economics (2006): "A high-reputation, established eBay dealer sold matched pairs of lots -- batches of vintage postcards -- under his regular identity and under new seller identities ... The difference in buyers' willingness-to-pay was 8.1% of the selling price." https://presnick.people.si.umich.edu/papers/postcards/index.html
- Rule 2. Lucking-Reiley, "Vickrey Auctions in Practice", Journal of Economic Perspectives (2000): in an English auction the winner pays "a price equal to the second-highest bidder's value (plus one bid increment)". http://www.davidreiley.com/papers/VickreyHistory.pdf
- Rule 3. Consumer Affairs Victoria, "Selling property by auction": "The lowest price a seller is prepared to accept for their property at auction ... is called the seller's reserve price"; "If bids do not meet your reserve ... the property will be 'passed in'." https://www.consumer.vic.gov.au/housing/buying-and-selling-property/selling-property/selling-property-by-auction
- Rule 4. Roth, HBS Working Knowledge (2005), on Roth & Ockenfels, AER 2002: Amazon auctions "can't end until at least 10 minutes have passed without a bid", so "other bidders always have 10 minutes to respond". https://hbswk.hbs.edu/item/amazon-ebay-and-the-bidding-wars
- Rule 5. Lebanese Code of Civil Procedure, Art. 973 (every bidder first deposits an amount equal to the opening price, or a bank guarantee) and Art. 977: "يجوز لكل من اشترك بالمزايدة وقام بموجب الايداع او الكفالة ولم يكن الزائد الاخير ان يسحب المال المودع او الكفالة فور انتهاء جلسة المزايدة" (any bidder who made the deposit and was not the last bidder may withdraw it as soon as the session ends). http://legiliban.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=260008&lawId=244565
