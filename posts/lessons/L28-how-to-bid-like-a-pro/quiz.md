# Story quiz (lesson 28)

**What's the first thing to decide before you bid?**
- How fast to bid
- Your maximum ✓
- The seller's reserve
- When to join

Extra: in an open auction the winner usually pays about one step above the runner-up's limit, not their own maximum (Vickrey; Lucking-Reiley 2000). And in Lebanese court auctions, bidders who don't win can withdraw their deposit as soon as the session ends (CCP Art. 977).
