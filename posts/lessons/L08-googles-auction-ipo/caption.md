# Lesson 08 captions

## Option 1

Google sold its first shares at an auction. Anyone could bid. 📈

In August 2004, instead of letting a bank offer the shares to big investors only, Google ran an online auction.
Anyone could bid over the internet, by fax or by phone, from just 5 shares.
Google had estimated $108 to $135 a share. The bids came in lower: $85.
The sale still raised $1.67 billion, and the next day the stock closed at $100.34.
When anyone can bid, the price is set in the open.

Sources: Los Angeles Times, CNN Money and The Washington Times (19 Aug 2004); Berkeley Technology Law Journal (2005).
Would you have bid at $85? 👇
