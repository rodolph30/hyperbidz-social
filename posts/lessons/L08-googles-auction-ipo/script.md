# Lesson 08 · Google's auction IPO · style v3 · quick · 37.4 s · am_michael 1.08 · music: ambient, seed 8

- In 2004, Google sold its first shares at an online auction.
- And the bids brought a surprise.
- Usually, a bank invites only big investors to bid on a new stock.
- Google let anyone bid online, from just five shares.
- Google had estimated up to $135 a share.
- The bids came in lower: $85.
- The sale still raised $1.67 billion.
- Next day, the stock closed at $100.34.
- When anyone can bid, the price is set in the open. Even for Google.
- Follow for more interesting auction stories.
