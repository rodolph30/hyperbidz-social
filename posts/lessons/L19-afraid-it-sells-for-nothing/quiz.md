# Lesson 19 quiz (optional story sticker)

**Question:** What's a reserve price?
- The first bid
- **The lowest price the seller will accept** ✅
- The highest bid allowed
- The auctioneer's fee

Extra: "The lowest price a seller is prepared to accept for their property at auction… is called the seller's reserve price." If bids don't meet it, the property is "passed in" and the highest bidder has the first right to negotiate (Consumer Affairs Victoria).
