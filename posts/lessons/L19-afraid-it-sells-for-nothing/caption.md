# Lesson 19 captions

## Option 1
What if someone bids $10 and takes your car? 🚗

That's the fear that keeps most sellers away from auctions.
But a real auction has a floor: the reserve.
It's the lowest price the seller is prepared to accept.
If the bids don't reach it, nothing sells. In Australia they call it "passed in".
The top bidder only gets the first chance to negotiate.

Source: Consumer Affairs Victoria, "Selling property by auction". The car, the $10 bid and the bar heights in the video are illustrative.
A reserve is a floor, not a ceiling. Did you know auctions worked this way? 👇
