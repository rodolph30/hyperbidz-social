Over 20,000 used cars. One auction. 🚗

In Japan, it's how dealers price ordinary used cars.

Its Tokyo site got so busy, it reached the limit of its size.

Source: USS Co., FY2025 results (Apr 2025 – Mar 2026) and auction data. Car icons and the example bids in the video are illustrative.
